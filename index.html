Who We Are

Pioneers in Heavy Industrial Fabrication

SARAV Fabrication is a premier name in heavy-duty engineering and metal fabrication. For over 15 years, we have delivered top-grade structural solutions, specializing in industrial sheds, fiber roofing, automated safety gates, and complex custom metal assemblies.

We pride ourselves on using high-tensile steel, maintaining robust safety standards, and ensuring our structures withstand heavy loads and harsh weather conditions. From Ahmedabad to the far reaches of Gujarat, we support businesses with structural integrity they can build their future on.
