Our Services

Industrial Shed

Our heavy-duty industrial sheds are engineered to withstand extreme weather conditions, high wind speeds, and heavy utility loads. We design, manufacture, and erect structures that maximize usable floor space and provide superior durability. Using high-tensile steel columns, precisely engineered truss systems, and advanced roof sheet profiling, we ensure your warehouse or factory is safe and efficient.

Every shed project is tailored to client specifications, incorporating crane girders, ventilation systems, polycarbonate daylight panels, and mezzanine floors where required. Our certified erection crew ensures safe and timely assembly on-site, adhering to rigorous structural design standards.

Custom Truss Engineering
Wind & Load Certified

Fiber Shed & Roofing

We provide high-quality fiber roofing and shed solutions that offer excellent transparency, lightweight properties, and exceptional resistance to atmospheric factors. Designed using high-performance FRP (Fiber Reinforced Plastic) and polycarbonate panels, our fiber sheds are perfect for letting in natural daylight while protecting goods and personnel from harsh UV rays.

These systems are highly cost-effective, easy to maintain, and completely rust-proof. Ideal for manufacturing plants, intermediate storage areas, warehouses, walkways, and parking lots where natural light reduces electricity overheads.

Industrial Security Gates

Our heavy-duty entrance gates are engineered for reliability and long-term security of industrial premises. Fabricated with thick steel beams and reinforced channels, these gates are built to resist external impact and operate smoothly under high-frequency daily usage cycles.

We offer automated sliding configurations, heavy double swing mechanisms, and custom security designs integrated with security sensors. The rollers, tracks, and locking gears are designed to prevent sag and ensure smooth alignment for years.

Bari Darvaja (Large Industrial Doors)

Specially designed heavy doors for factory bay openings, machine depots, and primary warehouses. These doors utilize thick-gauge sheets and steel reinforcement bars to offer unmatched security and resistance against unauthorized entry and forced intrusion.

Available in customized single or double-leaf setups, they can be designed to include ventilation ports, glass viewer slots, and heavy-duty mechanical locking bars. Perfect for warehouse loading bays and high-security manufacturing zones.

Katheda (Industrial Safety Railings)

Safety is paramount in any industrial workspace. We manufacture and install premium safety railings, stairs handrails, mezzanine barrier fences, and pathway guardrails designed to comply with heavy industrial safety guidelines. Built from structural steel, galvanized iron pipes, or heavy MS sections, our safety grids ensure secure operations at heights.

Anchored deep into the concrete floors using expanding bolts or welded to structural beams, these railings provide high load resistance against side falls and heavy impact, preventing workspace injuries.

Code-compliant heights
High lateral-load resistance
Deep base anchorage
Seamless handrail joints

Precision Welding Services

We provide premium-grade welding services for high-stress joints and critical structural reinforcements. Our crew is experienced in heavy-duty Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW/Stick), Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW/MIG), and Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW/TIG) methods suitable for mild steel, stainless steel, and alloy work.

We maintain precision alignment, control heat input to prevent distortion, and ensure deep weld penetration. Ideal for structural framing, pipeline racks, heavy machine bases, and structural repairing.

Arc, MIG & TIG capabilities
Deep penetration weld joints
Non-destructive weld testing
Expert structural repairs
